About me

My work has always sat at the intersection of logic, programming and languages. What motivates me is building things people actually use: listening to what they need, and shaping the tool around it. I like figuring out why something works or doesn't, and understanding what's really going on inside the systems I build. I take the less visible parts seriously: clean data and well-organised pipelines are what make a model useful.
